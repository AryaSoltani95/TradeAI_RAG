# TradingAI Culture

## Our Values

**1. Honest pricing.** We show the inspection report, the price history, and the reasoning behind every price. No hidden fees.

**2. Inspect properly.** A car is only ready for sale when our technicians would buy it for their own family.

**3. Own the outcome.** Whoever takes a customer's call also sees the deal through, from test drive to registration transfer.

**4. Learn from the data.** We test ideas against real sales and pricing data before we roll them out.

**5. Treat people well.** Staff, customers, and suppliers all get the same respect. We do not pressure buyers into finance they do not need.

## How We Work

- **Hybrid teams:** Sales advisers, technicians, and pricing analysts sit in the same project teams for each showroom launch and each pricing change.
- **Weekly stand-up:** Every branch holds a 15-minute stand-up on Monday mornings to review stock, complaints, and targets.
- **Monthly all-hands:** Held at the Manchester HQ or streamed to the other branches, with an update from the CEO and a review of Trustpilot feedback.
- **Open pricing reviews:** Pricing decisions are documented and visible to the whole company in our internal wiki.

## Recognition and Milestones

- 2016: First full year of profit after opening the Manchester workshop.
- 2019: Sheffield showroom opened, with a team transferred from Manchester to start it.
- 2021: TradingAI Price Index launched, developed by our pricing and data team.
- 2023: Named Regional Used Car Dealer of the Year, North West Automotive Awards.
- 2024: Passed 21,000 vehicles sold in a single year.

## Staff Life

- **Training:** Every new technician completes a 12-week induction, followed by annual inspection certification.
- **Wellbeing:** Access to a free employee assistance programme, and a mental health first-aider at every branch.
- **Flexibility:** Office and pricing roles allow hybrid working (three office days per week). Sales and workshop roles are on site.
- **Social:** Quarterly team lunches, an annual summer event, and a charity car wash each spring for a local food bank.

## Diversity and Inclusion

We aim for our teams to reflect the communities we serve. Our current workforce includes 41% women, 18% people from ethnic minority backgrounds, and 9 apprentices. We review pay gaps annually and publish the results in our internal report.

## Feedback

Staff can raise concerns through their line manager, the HR team (hr@tradingai.co.uk), or the confidential whistleblowing line, which is run by an external provider.
