## Founding Story

Daniel Harrow spent eight years working as a sales manager at a franchised dealership in Stockport. He noticed that many customers left the showroom distrustful of used car pricing, and that good cars were often sold at auction for less than they were worth, because buyers could not see their history. In 2014 he and Thomas Whitfield, a former workshop manager, opened a small forecourt in Trafford Park with a clear rule: every car gets a full inspection and every customer sees the report.

## Growth

The first years were slow. Growth came from repeat customers and referrals. Opening the Workshop in 2016 allowed TradingAI to recondition vehicles in-house, which reduced costs and improved quality. The move into finance in 2018 allowed more customers to buy the cars they wanted, and the Price Index in 2021 gave our pricing team a consistent method across all branches.

## Ownership

TradingAI Motors Ltd. is privately owned. Daniel Harrow and Thomas Whitfield hold the majority of shares. In 2020, a minority investment of 15% was made by Northgate Capital Partners, a Manchester-based growth investor, to fund the Sheffield and Liverpool openings.

## Looking Ahead

Our next focus is the commercial vehicle market, where we plan to extend the Price Index and open a dedicated commercial showroom in Birmingham by 2026.
