# TradingAI Overview

**TradingAI Motors Ltd.** is an independent used and nearly new car trading company based in Manchester, United Kingdom. Founded in 2014, we buy and sell inspected vehicles across four locations in the North of England: Manchester, Leeds, Sheffield, and Liverpool.

We do not manufacture cars. We earn from the margin between buying a vehicle and selling it, plus fees for financing arrangements, extended warranties, and vehicle preparation.

## What We Do

1. **Buy:** We source vehicles from private sellers, public auctions, leasing companies, and rental fleet disposals.
2. **Inspect:** Every car passes our 142-point inspection and is reconditioned in our Manchester workshop.
3. **Price:** Each listing is benchmarked against live auction and dealer data through the TradingAI Price Index.
4. **Sell:** Customers buy in person, online, or through a finance agreement with one of our partner lenders.
5. **Support:** Every car includes a 12-month TradingAI Assured warranty, with optional extended cover up to 36 months.

## Our Product Range

- **Nearly New:** under 2 years old, under 15,000 miles
- **Used Premium:** German and Japanese premium brands, 3 to 7 years old
- **Budget Used:** popular city models, 5 to 10 years old
- **Commercial Light:** vans and pickups up to 3.5 tonnes
- **Electric and Hybrid:** BEV and PHEV models, all ages
