## Mission

To make buying and selling used cars simple, transparent, and fair by pairing rigorous vehicle inspection with clear pricing and honest customer service.

## Vision

To become the most trusted independent car trading company in the North of England by 2030, and to lead the adoption of data-driven pricing in the used car market.

## Business Model

TradingAI is an independent automotive trading company. It does not manufacture vehicles. Its revenue comes from the margin between vehicle purchase and resale, plus fees for financing arrangements, extended warranties, and vehicle preparation.

Core activities:

- **Vehicle purchasing:** buying used cars from private sellers, at public auctions (Manheim UK and BCA), from leasing companies, and from rental fleet disposals.
- **Vehicle sales:** selling inspected used cars to retail customers, and new cars on behalf of franchised dealers under agency agreements.
- **Inspection and reconditioning:** a 142-point inspection, mechanical repair, bodywork, and valeting in our Manchester workshop.
- **Financing and part-exchange:** arranging hire purchase and personal contract purchase through partner lenders, including Close Brothers Motor Finance and Startline Motor Finance, and accepting part-exchange vehicles.
- **After-sales:** a 12-month TradingAI Assured warranty as standard, optional extended cover up to 36 months, and help with V5C registration transfer.
