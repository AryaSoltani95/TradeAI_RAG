# Careers at TradingAI

TradingAI is hiring for roles in sales, workshop, pricing, and operations across our four locations. We look for people who are honest with customers, careful with detail, and happy to learn about cars and data.

## Why Work With Us

- Competitive basic salary, plus commission for sales roles
- 25 days of annual leave, rising to 28 after 3 years, plus bank holidays
- Pension scheme with 5% employer contribution
- Staff discount on vehicles and extended warranties
- Paid training, including IMI qualifications for technicians
- Employee assistance programme and mental health first-aider support

## Recruitment Process

1. **Application:** Apply through the role page on www.tradingai.co.uk/careers or email careers@tradingai.co.uk.
2. **Screening call:** A 20-minute call with our recruitment team (about one week after applying).
3. **Practical task:** Sales roles complete a role-play with a branch manager. Technician roles complete a short diagnostic test. Pricing roles complete a spreadsheet exercise.
4. **Final interview:** With the hiring manager and one peer from the team.
5. **Offer:** Usually within five working days of the final interview.

## Equal Opportunities

We welcome applications from everyone and make reasonable adjustments for candidates with disabilities at every stage. We do not ask for salary history.
