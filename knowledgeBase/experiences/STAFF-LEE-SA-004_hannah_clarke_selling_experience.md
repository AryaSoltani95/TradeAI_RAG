Strategy of Hannah Clarke for the Skoda Octavia Estate Mk4: ask how many people and how much luggage the car will carry each week.
Strategy of Hannah Clarke for the Skoda Octavia Estate Mk4: show the boot space with the rear seats folded and a child seat fitted.
Strategy of Hannah Clarke for the Skoda Octavia Estate Mk4: explain the TradingAI inspection report line by line, especially brake and tyre figures.
Strategy of Hannah Clarke for the Skoda Octavia Estate Mk4: compare the 1.5 TSI petrol and 2.0 TDI diesel on the customer's annual mileage before recommending either.
Strategy of Hannah Clarke for the Skoda Octavia Estate Mk4: offer TradingAI Assured 12-month cover at handover.
Customer request handled by Hannah Clarke for the Skoda Octavia Estate Mk4: "We need a car for the school run and weekend trips to the Dales."
Response of Hannah Clarke to the school-run request for the Skoda Octavia Estate Mk4: recommended the 1.5 TSI petrol with full service history and showed the rear seat folding for a pushchair.

Response of Hannah Clarke to the part-exchange request for the Skoda Octavia Estate Mk4: used the TradingAI Price Index and the inspection report, and did not promise more than the index supported.

## Experience 2: Toyota RAV4 Hybrid

Response of Hannah Clarke to the city-driving request for the Toyota RAV4 Hybrid: showed the fuel economy figures in town, and recommended the hybrid over the petrol RAV4 for that use.
