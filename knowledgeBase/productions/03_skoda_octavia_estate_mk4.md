# Skoda Octavia Estate Mk4 (2020-2024): Product Guide

## Summary

The Skoda Octavia Estate is an estate car sold by TradingAI Motors Ltd. in the Nearly New and Used Premium categories.
The Skoda Octavia Estate is one of the most practical estate cars on the used market, with the largest boot in its class and good value for money.
The Skoda Octavia Estate typical stock price is between £13,500 and £28,000.
The Skoda Octavia Estate typical mileage is between 12,000 and 55,000 miles.

## Available Engines

The Skoda Octavia Estate is available with the 1.0 TSI petrol engine.
The Skoda Octavia Estate is available with the 1.5 TSI petrol engine.
The Skoda Octavia Estate is available with the 2.0 TDI diesel engine.
The Skoda Octavia Estate is available with the vRS 2.0 TSI performance engine.
The Skoda Octavia Estate is available with the iV plug-in hybrid engine.

## Advantages of the Skoda Octavia Estate

Advantage of the Skoda Octavia Estate: a very large 640-litre boot, growing to 1,700 litres with the rear seats folded.
Advantage of the Skoda Octavia Estate: generous rear legroom, comparable to some executive cars.
Advantage of the Skoda Octavia Estate: practical touches such as umbrella storage, an ice scraper, and tidy-up tools.
Advantage of the Skoda Octavia Estate: good fuel economy on the 2.0 TDI and 1.5 TSI engines.
Advantage of the Skoda Octavia Estate: competitive prices and low depreciation for its class.
Advantage of the Skoda Octavia Estate: a comfortable ride on long motorway journeys.

## Disadvantages of the Skoda Octavia Estate

Disadvantage of the Skoda Octavia Estate: less prestige than German premium estates.
Disadvantage of the Skoda Octavia Estate: interior plastics feel firmer than those of the Volkswagen Golf.
Disadvantage of the Skoda Octavia Estate vRS: the firm suspension suits enthusiasts but not a relaxed ride.
Disadvantage of the Skoda Octavia Estate: some touch controls on the centre screen take practice to use.
Disadvantage of the Skoda Octavia Estate: not suitable for off-road use.
