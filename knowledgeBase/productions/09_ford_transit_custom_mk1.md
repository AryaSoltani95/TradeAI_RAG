# Ford Transit Custom Mk1 facelift (2018-2023): Product Guide

## Summary

The Ford Transit Custom is a light commercial van sold by TradingAI Motors Ltd. in the Commercial Light category.
The Ford Transit Custom is used by trades, couriers, and small businesses, with several body styles and strong reliability.
The Ford Transit Custom typical stock price is between £8,000 and £28,000.
The Ford Transit Custom typical mileage is between 25,000 and 120,000 miles.

## Available Engines

The Ford Transit Custom is available with the 2.0 EcoBlue diesel engine in 105 PS output.
The Ford Transit Custom is available with the 2.0 EcoBlue diesel engine in 170 PS output.

## Available Body Styles

The Ford Transit Custom is available as a panel van for goods transport.
The Ford Transit Custom is available as a crew van for team transport.
The Ford Transit Custom is available as a kombi with mixed passenger and load space.
The Ford Transit Custom is available as a Double Cab pickup for loads and towing.

## Advantages of the Ford Transit Custom

Advantage of the Ford Transit Custom: a wide range of body styles and load lengths.
Advantage of the Ford Transit Custom panel van: a large load volume of up to around 9 cubic metres on the long-wheelbase version.
Advantage of the Ford Transit Custom: good payload and towing capacity.
Advantage of the Ford Transit Custom: a comfortable driving position for long working days.
Advantage of the Ford Transit Custom: strong residual values and a wide dealer network.
Advantage of the Ford Transit Custom: efficient diesel engines for a vehicle of its size.

## Disadvantages of the Ford Transit Custom

Disadvantage of the Ford Transit Custom: poor fuel economy when fully loaded.
Disadvantage of the Ford Transit Custom: not suitable for passenger comfort on long journeys.
Disadvantage of the Ford Transit Custom: the ride can be firm when the van is empty.
Disadvantage of the Ford Transit Custom: higher road tax and insurance costs than cars.
Disadvantage of the Ford Transit Custom: some early models had DPF and EGR issues, so the service history matters.
