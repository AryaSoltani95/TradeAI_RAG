# Toyota RAV4 Hybrid 5th Generation (2019-2024): Product Guide

The Toyota RAV4 Hybrid is a compact SUV sold by TradingAI Motors Ltd. in the Used Premium and Electric and Hybrid categories.
The Toyota RAV4 Hybrid is a practical SUV with a strong hybrid system, good space, and a reputation for reliability.
The Toyota RAV4 Hybrid typical stock price is between £19,000 and £33,000.
The Toyota RAV4 Hybrid typical mileage is between 10,000 and 60,000 miles.

The Toyota RAV4 Hybrid is available with the 2.5 hybrid front-wheel-drive engine.
The Toyota RAV4 Hybrid is available with the 2.5 hybrid all-wheel-drive (AWD) engine.

## Advantages of the Toyota RAV4 Hybrid

Advantage of the Toyota RAV4 Hybrid: excellent fuel economy in town, often above 45 mpg in mixed driving.
Advantage of the Toyota RAV4 Hybrid: proven hybrid reliability with a long service history.
Advantage of the Toyota RAV4 Hybrid: a high driving position and good visibility for the driver.
Advantage of the Toyota RAV4 Hybrid AWD: good grip on wet roads, snow, and light gravel.
Advantage of the Toyota RAV4 Hybrid: a spacious 542-litre boot with a flat load floor.
Advantage of the Toyota RAV4 Hybrid: strong resale value compared with petrol SUVs.
