# Mercedes-Benz E-Class (W213), 2016–2023

**Category:** Used Premium
**Body:** 4-door saloon, estate (S213)
**Engines:** E200, E220d, E300 plug-in hybrid, E53 AMG
**Typical stock price (GBP):** £18,500 – £36,000
**Typical mileage:** 20,000 – 70,000

## Overview

The Mercedes-Benz E-Class is a luxury executive car known for its comfort, refined cabin, and strong safety features. It suits customers who want an elegant, comfortable car for long journeys and business use.

## Advantages

- Exceptionally comfortable ride and quiet cabin
- High-quality materials and a refined, elegant interior
- Strong technology, including a large digital cockpit on later models
- Excellent safety features and driver assistance systems
- Spacious rear seats with good legroom
- Good residual values in the premium segment

## Disadvantages

- Expensive to service and repair, especially outside the warranty period
- Some electrical faults reported on early W213 cars, so history checks are important
- Infotainment (COMAND) can be slow to respond on older models
- High insurance costs on AMG models
- Heavier than rivals, which affects economy on the petrol engines

## Compatibility With Customer Requests

**Suggest when the customer asks for:**
- Elegant luxury car
- Comfortable long-distance cruiser
- Executive or chauffeur-style car
- Quiet and refined cabin
- Strong safety features
- Estate version for space and prestige (S213)

**Do not suggest when the customer asks for:**
- Off-road usage
- Cheapest or most economic car
- Low insurance cost (especially the AMG)
- Sporty handling as the main priority (the BMW 3 Series is sharper)
- Small city car

## Staff Guidance

1. Ask whether the car is for executive travel, family use, or personal comfort.
2. For a long-distance customer, suggest the E220d and demonstrate the seat comfort on a motorway test drive.
3. Check the full service history and any electrical fault records before the sale, as these are key for this model.
4. Explain that servicing costs are higher than mainstream brands and offer the 36-month extended warranty.
5. For the E300 plug-in, check the battery health report and explain the company car benefit.
