# Volkswagen Golf Mk8 (2020-2024): Product Guide

## Summary

The Volkswagen Golf Mk8 is a 5-door hatchback sold by TradingAI Motors Ltd. in the Nearly New and Used Premium categories.
It is a balanced, practical family hatchback with a refined cabin, good driving manners, and strong resale value.
Typical stock price is between £14,500 and £26,000, with mileage between 8,000 and 45,000 miles.

## Available Engines

The Volkswagen Golf Mk8 is available with the 1.0 TSI petrol engine.
The Volkswagen Golf Mk8 is available with the 1.5 eTSI mild hybrid engine.
The Volkswagen Golf Mk8 is available with the 2.0 TDI diesel engine.
The Volkswagen Golf Mk8 is available with the GTI 2.0 TSI performance engine.
The Volkswagen Golf Mk8 is available with the GTE plug-in hybrid engine.

## Advantages of the Volkswagen Golf Mk8

The Volkswagen Golf Mk8 has solid build quality and a quiet, mature cabin.
The Volkswagen Golf Mk8 offers excellent motorway comfort and fuel economy on the 1.5 eTSI and 2.0 TDI engines.
The Volkswagen Golf Mk8 has a large boot of 381 litres and flexible rear seats.
The Volkswagen Golf Mk8 achieved a Euro NCAP 5-star safety rating in 2019.
The Volkswagen Golf Mk8 has low ownership costs and wide parts availability.
The Volkswagen Golf Mk8 offers good value on the used market compared with premium hatchbacks.

## Disadvantages of the Volkswagen Golf Mk8

The Volkswagen Golf Mk8 has touch-sensitive steering wheel and dashboard controls that some drivers find distracting.
The Volkswagen Golf Mk8 has a firm ride on 18-inch and larger wheels.
The Volkswagen Golf Mk8 has adequate but not generous rear legroom for three adults.
The Volkswagen Golf Mk8 GTI and GTE have higher insurance groups and higher servicing costs than other versions.
The Volkswagen Golf Mk8 is not designed for off-road use or for towing loads over 1,500 kg.

## Customer Request Matching: Do Not Recommend the Volkswagen Golf Mk8

Do not recommend the Volkswagen Golf Mk8 when the customer asks for off-road usage.
Do not recommend the Volkswagen Golf Mk8 when the customer asks to tow a trailer over 1,500 kg.
Do not recommend the Volkswagen Golf Mk8 when the customer asks for the cheapest car available.
Do not recommend the Volkswagen Golf Mk8 when the customer asks for a spacious SUV or a high driving position.
